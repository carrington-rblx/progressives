The future is a choice

What would you do if you were President?

Every round presents 25 proposals selected from a balanced mix of 15 public-policy categories. You can reject an idea, suggest an improvement, keep it, or say you don't know. No name or email is requested.

Four ways to respond

Your judgement. Your choice.

01 / REJECT

I Reject

Not the direction you would take? Reject the proposal.

02 / IMPROVE

I Improve

See a better way? Write a short suggestion.

03 / KEEP

I Keep

Support the proposal and keep it as presented.

04 / UNDECIDED

I Don't Know

Not enough information or no clear view? Say so.

Before you begin · 25 proposals

Make every choice count.

You'll see one proposal at a time. Read the summary, open “Learn more” for context, then choose how you feel. Your round is anonymous: we don't ask for your name, email address or account details.

How to respond

← I Reject · ↑ I Improve · → I Keep · ↓ I Don't Know

You can also use the four buttons below each card. An improvement must include a short written explanation. Your answers are submitted only when you complete all 25 proposals; unfinished rounds are not saved.
