Privacy notice & participation terms

Version 1.2 · Last updated November 3, 2026

Important: The platform asks for opinions on public policy. Your responses may reveal political opinions, which are sensitive personal data. You do not have to participate. The participation form is only unlocked after you actively give explicit consent.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

The AmentAmour campaign team operates this participation experience and is responsible for the information submitted through it. For privacy questions or requests, contact campaign@electament.com.

2. What information is collected?

  • Your choices on 25 You President policy proposals (Reject, Improve, Keep or I Don’t Know), including the written suggestions you submit when you choose Improve.
  • Any separate ideas or submissions collected through other participation features, where enabled.
  • A record of your explicit consent, including the time it was given and the version of this notice. For You President, this consent is collected before the game begins.

You President does not ask for a name, username, account or email address. A random response reference is created when a completed round is submitted so administrators can review that round without identifying the participant. The game does not intentionally store a name, email address, account ID or device fingerprint with answers. Website, hosting and database infrastructure may process technical connection data in their operational logs. Do not include your real name, contact details or other private information in improvement suggestions.

3. Why is it used?

Submissions are used to understand participation, produce aggregated statistics, review suggestions and help the campaign understand which proposals participants want to keep, improve or reject. Submissions may also be reviewed by authorised campaign team members for moderation and administration. We do not publish individual responses as public profiles.

4. Political opinions and explicit consent

Choices and comments about public policy may reveal political opinions. You President requires an affirmative, unticked-by-default consent checkbox before a participant can begin. The consent record is associated with the completed game record and this notice version. Consent is voluntary; if you do not consent, you cannot start or submit a You President round. You may withdraw consent for future processing by contacting the campaign at the address above. Withdrawal does not make processing that took place before withdrawal invalid.

5. Where information is stored and who can access it?

You President responses are stored in the campaign's Supabase-hosted database. The database stores a random response reference, completion time, proposal decisions and any improvement text, but no participant name, email address or account identifier. Access to individual answer sets and improvement suggestions is restricted to authorised campaign administrators. Public visitors can read only active proposal text; they cannot read submitted responses. Supabase provides the underlying database infrastructure as a service provider.

6. How long is information kept?

Submissions are retained while the campaign is active. When the campaign ends, the campaign intends to review the records for deletion or anonymisation, subject to any applicable legal, regulatory or record-keeping requirements. There is not currently an automated deletion schedule published for these records. You may contact the campaign to ask about a submission or request deletion.

7. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to participate, leave optional comment fields blank, or withdraw consent for future processing. You can contact campaign@electament.com with questions or requests concerning access, correction or deletion. We may need enough information to identify the relevant submission, but please do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents by email.

8. Children and young participants

The campaign should confirm the age eligibility rules and any additional parental-consent requirements that apply before promoting this experience to minors. If you are unsure whether you can consent, do not submit responses.

9. Participation terms

  • Participation is voluntary and does not require an account, username, password or email address.
  • Do not include identifying or private information in your responses.
  • Keep comments and ideas respectful. Do not submit threats, harassment, unlawful content or another person's private information.
  • Submissions are suggestions and expressions of opinion, not a promise that the campaign will adopt a proposal.
  • The campaign may moderate or remove submissions that breach these participation rules.
  • This is an independent campaign participation experience.

10. Changes to this notice

If the way submissions are collected or used changes materially, the campaign should update this notice and obtain any new consent required before continuing the affected processing. The notice version shown on the consent screen identifies the terms participants agreed to.
